How it works. Every hand each fly antes and gets 2 hole cards. Then across four streets — pre-flop, flop, turn, river — the board is dealt out and, one fly at a time, each still-in fly's mushroom body reads its currently visible hand strength into a sparse Kenyon-cell code and picks fold / call / raise. Survivors show down; best 5-of-7 hand wins the pot. The chips won or lost fire dopamine or pain and rewrite the KC→action weights across every decision that fly made — so flies learn to fold weak boards and press strong ones, with no rules given. ·Real deck, streets & hand rankings; simplified fixed-bet action. A behavioral model of the FlyGambler connectome brain — for fun, not gambling advice.
